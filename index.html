Selected work

01 — Community Leadership

Art, connection, and community

In Kunshan, I founded a community art initiative that brings creative activities and materials to autistic children through local partnerships.

Since 2024, the project has grown through collaboration with a rehabilitation center and support from art-materials partner Pébéo.

02 — Data Visualization

A Shot of Hope

A data visualization project exploring the relationship between COVID-19 vaccination and global trends in cases and deaths.

Using Tableau and data from Our World in Data, I created four visualizations to explore changes over time, compare countries, and place key vaccine rollout milestones alongside pandemic trends.

About me

Curious about people.
Drawn to stories.

I'm Ruohan Qu, an Information Science student at UW–Madison pursuing a certificate in Digital Studies. Originally from the Kunshan area of China, I bring an interest in people, places, and everyday experiences to my work.

My goal is to pursue a career in UX, with a particular interest in UX research and human–computer interaction. Whether through community work, photography, animation, or research, I enjoy observing, listening, and finding ways to make people's experiences more understandable and meaningful.

Outside of class, I practice kendo, take photographs, and enjoy reading and drawing.

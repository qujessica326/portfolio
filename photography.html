Documentary photography · Xiemaqiao, China · Ongoing project

Everyday life in Xiemaqiao

Xiemaqiao is an old town undergoing commercial redevelopment. Through photographs and conversations, I explore the relationship between its visitor-facing spaces and the everyday lives of local residents. The project asks how this transformation affects the people who call the town home, and seeks to make their experiences more visible.

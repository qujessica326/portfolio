Art, connection, and community

A community art initiative for autistic children, built through local relationships and a shared belief in creative expression.

My role

Founder

Started

Location

Kunshan, China

The story so far

Growing, year by year.

2024

Where it began

After taking a course in Rehabilitation Psychology and Special Education, I began to think about how my interest in drawing could connect with what I had learned. In July 2024, I founded a community art initiative in Kunshan to create opportunities for autistic children to explore art.

I began building a partnership with a local rehabilitation center, bringing together people and resources around a shared idea: making space for creative expression.

2025

Turning an idea into a classroom

Art activities began at the rehabilitation center. Preparing materials and working with our partners helped turn the idea into a place where children could make and explore. Pébéo supported the initiative by providing art materials.

2026 — Today

A space that continues to grow

In 2026, a dedicated classroom was established at the rehabilitation center. Since then, many autistic children have taken part in the art activities. Seeing the space used by children and their community has made the project’s growth especially meaningful to me.

The initiative continues through relationships with the center and its supporters, and I hope to keep learning how to make it sustainable over time.
